# Open-Source Data Visualization Methodological Atlas

> **Core Thesis**  
> In an era where AI generates endless charts in three seconds, the scarcest human capability is **visual literacy**—the power to perceive structural order beneath noisy reality, choose honest encodings, and translate open-source geometric engines into genuine human insight.

---

## I. Five Classical Methodological Foundations

Before writing a single line of code, every installation inside *The Pantheon of Order* honors five foundational frameworks:

1. **From Data to Viz** (*Yan Holtz*) — A strict decision tree driven by **input data topology** (numeric, categorical, temporal, relational, spatial), preventing structural mismatches and cataloging traps such as spaghetti lines or overplotting.
2. **Visual Vocabulary** (*Financial Times — Alan Smith et al.*) — A taxonomy driven by **analytical communication intent**: *Deviation, Correlation, Ranking, Distribution, Change over Time, Part-to-Whole, Spatial, Flow*.
3. **Data Viz Project** (*Ferdio*) — A morphological lexicon indexing non-standard geometric encodings beyond generic bar and pie templates.
4. **Data Humanism** (*Giorgia Lupi*) — Rejects cold statistical flattening whenever human nuance matters, encoding lived experience and individuality into bespoke visual glyphs.
5. **Data Sketches & Scrollytelling** (*Nadieh Bremer, Shirley Wu & The Pudding*) — Proves that custom mathematical geometry and choreographed visual transitions elevate empirical data into museum-grade essays.

## II. Open-Source Engine Selection Matrix

| Engine / Kernel | Core Paradigm | Unrivaled Sweet Spot | Avoid Using When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D3.js** | Low-level Data-to-DOM & Mathematical Scales | Bespoke art-grade geometry: hierarchical edge bundling, custom radial astrolabes, alluvial flows, spring physics | Routine corporate CRUD dashboards prioritizing five-minute configuration |
| **Apache ECharts** | High-performance Declarative Canvas / WebGL | High-density operational telemetry, interactive brushing/zooming, heat calendars, large time series | Breaking coordinate rules completely for organic experimental sculpture |
| **Vega-Lite** | Declarative Grammar of Graphics | Reproducible research, rapid statistical faceting, side-by-side hypothesis validation | Theatrical particle simulations or bespoke spring kinematics |
| **deck.gl** | GPU-Accelerated WebGL2 Spatial Layers | Millions of GPS arcs, commuter pulses, 3D hexagon density columns, global logistics | Non-spatial relational data forcibly wrapped inside geographic shells |
| **AntV G2 / G6** | Design Grammar + Graph Topology Kernel | Multi-tier supply chains, data lineage graphs, combo hulls, disciplined product visuals | Small conceptual diagrams with fewer than ten entities |
| **RAWGraphs** | Zero-code Client-Side Vector Studio | Rapidly exporting messy CSVs into editable SVG alluvials, circle packs, or streamgraphs | Live streaming applications wired to telemetry websockets |
| **Observable Plot** | Concise Exploratory Grammar | Rapid data journalism prototyping, beeswarm plots, faceted distribution audits | Full-screen atmospheric spatial art stages |
| **Three.js / WebGL** | 3D Scene Graph & Custom Shaders | High-dimensional embedding galaxies, navigable point clouds, spatial twins | Quantitative 2D financial bars where perspective foreshortening lies |

## IV. Five Visual Deceptions of the AI Era

1. **The Tyranny of the Mean** — Averaging a bimodal distribution invents a phantom "typical user" experienced by almost nobody. *Correction: Reveal every specimen via beeswarms or ridgelines.*
2. **Truncated Axis Panic** — Starting a bar baseline at 95% instead of 0% dramatizes a 1% flutter as a catastrophic cliff. *Correction: Anchor length encodings strictly at zero.*
3. **Dual-Axis Causality Mirages** — Independently rescaling unrelated Y-axes makes any two arbitrary curves appear correlated. *Correction: Plot indexed relative percentages or connected scatterpaths.*
4. **Hairball Network Mysticism** — Dumping hundreds of crossing straight edges creates intimidation with zero analytical readability. *Correction: Enforce hierarchical edge bundling and interactive node isolation.*
5. **3D Perspective Distortion** — Tilting pie wedges or bars makes foreground values appear double equal slices in the rear. *Correction: Reserve three dimensions strictly for genuinely volumetric geometry.*
